📖 Fairness Metrics Glossary

Subgroup AUC

Measures discrimination ability strictly within comments that mention a sensitive identity.

BPSN AUC (Background Positive, Subgroup Negative)

Measures whether non-toxic comments mentioning the identity are falsely flagged as toxic. Higher BPSN means identity words are not penalized.

BNSP AUC (Background Negative, Subgroup Positive)

Measures whether toxic comments mentioning the identity are appropriately detected.

Equalized Odds / FPR Parity

Ensures that innocent comments from every demographic group have an equal probability of remaining unflagged.
